Streaming Data & Operations · case study

What makes a feed incident end in an SLA breach?

Simply.TV delivers metadata and EPG to platforms on every continent. When a feed arrives incomplete, late or with a changed schema, someone has to diagnose it before the SLA expires. This analysis measures how often that happens, why, and what can be anticipated.

A feed can deliver "OK" and still arrive truncated. That case was hunted with IsolationForest and a completeness rule adjusted for each feed's weekly pattern, and validated against the simulation ground truth.
